🏡 Supporting Your Child at Home

  • Externalize Routines: Hang visual picture checklists near study spaces and bedrooms.
  • Transition Cues: Provide 5-minute and 2-minute gentle warnings before switching tasks.
  • Co-Regulation First: During sensory overload, offer quiet comfort before problem-solving.
  • Activity Ideas: Try 10-minute Memory Match rounds or Visual Math dot puzzles together.

📥 Downloadable Guides & Advocacy

Download a summary guide for IEP/504 school meetings or print directly.

🏫 Inclusive Classroom Practices

  • Chunked Directions: Display numbered steps visibly on the board.
  • Multimodal Output: Allow speech-to-text, audio books, or visual mind-maps.
  • Sensory Zoning: Create a low-glare reading corner with noise-reducing headphones.

🔒 About NeuroCare & Local Privacy

NeuroCare is an inclusive web project built with pure HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript to support neurodivergent learners and families.

Privacy Commitment: Your to-do items, mood logs, journal entries, schedules, and accessibility settings are saved locally in your browser's localStorage. No data is transmitted to external servers.

Medical Notice: NeuroCare is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment. The AI Assistant and Community Hub are local demonstrations.
